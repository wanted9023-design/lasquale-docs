# Step 10: Extra Spoofing

#### Instructions for Using DeviceCleanup

1. **Unpack the Files**
   * Extract the contents of `DeviceCleanup.zip` to a folder on your computer.
2. **Run the Cleanup Script**
   * Locate `Start_DeviceCleanup.bat` in the extracted folder.
   * Right-click on the file and select **Run as administrator**.
3. **Restart Your PC**
   * Restart your computer to apply the changes

---

### Monitor Spoofing

1. **Download CRU**
2. If your monitor supports G-Sync, **disable it** in the NVIDIA Control Panel before proceeding.
3. Extract the contents of `cru-1.5.3.zip` to a folder on your computer.
4. Locate the `CRU.exe` in the extracted folder.
   * Right click on the file and select **Run as administrator.**
5. Follow the video to change serials for all your monitors

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important Note on Editing Serials**

* **Only change a few characters** — replace **letter for letter** and **number for number**.
  ✅ Example: `Xka25` ➝ `Vga62`
  ❌ **Do NOT completely overwrite or randomize values like** `OT2374K`.
* If your **Serial Number** field is **empty**, **do not touch it**.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
If your monitor does go black; unplug the HDMI cable, restart your PC and replug-in the HDMI cable.
{% endhint %}
