# Precaution 1: Compatibility

{% hint style="warning" %}
### Supported Motherboards

* MSI
* ASUS (Use Compatibility Checker for more Information)
* ASRock
* Gigabyte
* HP (Manual only)
* Lenovo and Acer (DESKTOP ONLY)
* **Motherboard not listed here? We offer a lot more! Open a ticket and ask!**
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
### Internet connection

Due to MAC spoofing requirements, you must disable Wi-Fi and Bluetooth in BIOS. If you rely on Wi-Fi as your primary connection, you'll need to purchase a new Wi-Fi adapter before spoofing. If your MAC address remains unchanged after spoofing, please open a ticket and report the issue.
{% endhint %}
